# DJ Booth Card — Do-NOTs + Quick Fixes

Keep this by the mixer. Use it to avoid 90% of bad mixes.

## Harmonic (Key)
- Don’t: Mix unrelated keys cold (e.g., 8A → 1B) over a long blend.
- Do: Transition at breaks with echo-out, or stick to same/relative/±1 Camelot; +2 for intentional lift.
- Don’t: Stack two strong vocals in clashing keys.
- Do: Ride instrumental/drum tool under the incoming vocal.

## Phrasing & Rhythm
- Don’t: Drop the new kick off-phrase (e.g., on bar 7 of 8).
- Do: Count 16/32; loop to land on the “1.”
- Don’t: Blend busy over busy sections.
- Do: Busy → simple (hook over drums, drums under hook).

## Lows & Sub
- Don’t: Run two basslines at once.
- Do: One-bass rule — swap lows on the first downbeat of an 8/16.
- Don’t: Cut lows on both decks (hollow drop).
- Do: Keep lows on exactly one deck at all times.

## Gain & Dynamics
- Don’t: Redline to “add energy.”
- Do: Gain-stage (peaks in green/yellow); use EQ/filters for lift.
- Don’t: Let a quiet master creep up, then slam a +6 dB jump.
- Do: Match channel gains before opening the fader.

## EQ & Filters
- Don’t: Twist all three EQs at once (phasey mush).
- Do: Move one band decisively; small, purposeful changes.
- Don’t: Crank high-resonance filter while boosting highs.
- Do: Low-res sweeps; if resonance is high, keep highs flat/down.

## Tempo & Sync
- Don’t: Force long blends with >6% BPM difference.
- Do: Short, impact transitions or breakdown cuts; or step BPM gradually.
- Don’t: Trust warp/beatgrids blindly.
- Do: Nudge with your ear; set/verify grids & cue points ahead of time.

## FX & Tricks
- Don’t: Layer heavy reverb/echo tails over a busy drop.
- Do: Tails into silence or filtered beds; kill FX before the new kick.
- Don’t: “FX to fix” bad key/phrase choices.
- Do: Bail clean — echo-out on the 1, cut, reset.

## Library & Prep
- Don’t: Walk on stage with untagged, unknown intros/outros.
- Do: Tag key (Camelot), BPM, intro/outro bars, energy (1–10), vocal presence.
- Don’t: Ignore the room’s arc.
- Do: Plan a curve (build → peak → breathe) and pick tracks that serve it.

## Crowd & Flow
- Don’t: Jump genre/feel mid-groove without a bridge.
- Do: Use a vocal tool, percussion loop, or breakdown to pivot.
- Don’t: Over-blend bangers (two big drops fighting).
- Do: Feature one hero at a time — tease, don’t smother.

## Emergency Fixes (when it’s going sideways)
- Key clash → Kill mids on one deck, echo the offender, cut on the 1.
- Bass clash → Kill lows on incoming, wait 8 bars, swap on the 1.
- Off-phrase → Loop 4/8 on one deck to realign.
- Volume jump → Pull channel gain −2 dB, then re-open.
- Too busy → Mute a band (often low-mids) on one deck to create space.
